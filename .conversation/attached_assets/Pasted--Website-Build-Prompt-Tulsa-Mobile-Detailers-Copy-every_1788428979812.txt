# Website Build Prompt — Tulsa Mobile Detailers

Copy everything below into your website builder (Lovable, v0, Bolt, etc.)

---

Build a clean, modern, professional website for a mobile car detailing business.

**Business name:** Tulsa Mobile Detailers
**Business description:** A mobile car detailing service based in Tulsa, OK, USA. We come to the customer — door-to-door detailing at their home or office, no need to drive anywhere.

**Overall design direction:**
- Clean, modern, minimal aesthetic — feels premium and professional, not cluttered
- Easy to navigate on both desktop and mobile
- Generous white space, clear typography hierarchy, smooth subtle animations (fade-ins, slide-ups on scroll, hover effects) — nothing flashy or distracting
- Color palette: dark charcoal/black + white, with one accent color (e.g. a deep blue or metallic silver/chrome) to reflect an automotive, high-end detailing feel
- Use high-quality automotive imagery/placeholders (shiny cars, close-up detailing shots)
- Sticky/simple navigation bar across all pages: Home, Gallery, Book Appointment, Contact

---

## Page 1: Home Page
- Hero section with a bold headline (e.g. "Tulsa's Premier Mobile Detailing — We Come To You") and a subheadline mentioning door-to-door service in Tulsa, OK. Include a clear "Book Now" call-to-action button.
- Animated "Our Services" section listing these services as cards or icons that animate in on scroll (fade/slide in one after another):
  1. Interior Detailing
  2. Exterior Detailing
  3. Ceramic Coating
  4. Rubbing & Polishing
- Each service card should have a short 1-2 sentence description and a small icon or image.
- A brief "Why Choose Us" section highlighting: mobile/door-to-door convenience, professional results, Tulsa-based, quality products.
- A closing call-to-action banner directing users to the Book Appointment page.
- Footer with business name, quick links, and social icons.

## Page 2: Gallery Page
- Clean grid layout showcasing "Before & After" transformations.
- Use a before/after slider or side-by-side comparison cards for each set of images.
- Include placeholder image pairs (at least 6-8 sets) that can later be replaced with real photos.
- Simple filter or tabs by category (Interior / Exterior / Ceramic Coating) is a nice-to-have.

## Page 3: Book Appointment Page
- Minimal, uncluttered booking form with fields for:
  - Name
  - Phone number
  - Email
  - Address (for the mobile service location)
  - Service needed (dropdown: Interior Detailing, Exterior Detailing, Ceramic Coating, Rubbing & Polishing)
  - Preferred date and time
  - Optional notes/message
- Clear "Submit Request" or "Book Now" button
- Reassuring confirmation message after submission
- Keep the page visually light — no clutter, just the form and a short intro line confirming this is for the Tulsa, OK service area

## Page 4: Contact Us Page
- Simple, clean layout with:
  - Business name and short tagline
  - Email address (placeholder: info@tulsamobiledetailers.com)
  - Social media icons/links: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok
  - Optional embedded map showing Tulsa, OK service area
  - Short note confirming service area (Tulsa, OK and surrounding areas)

---

**Technical notes:**
- Fully responsive (mobile-first)
- Smooth scroll animations throughout, especially on the Home page services section
- Fast-loading, modern font pairing (e.g. a clean sans-serif for body + a slightly bolder sans-serif for headings)
- Consistent header/footer across all 4 pages
